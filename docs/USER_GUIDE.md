# CareerMate AI — User Guide & Feature Manual

Welcome to **CareerMate AI**, Bangladesh's intelligent career development and job readiness platform. This manual walks you through using all modules effectively.

---

## 1. Authentication & Onboarding

### Account Registration & Login
1. Navigate to **Create Account** (`/accounts/register/`).
2. Provide your Name, Email, Password (min 8 characters), and select your role:
   - **University Student** (current undergraduate or post-graduate).
   - **Job Seeker / Fresh Graduate** (graduated and seeking junior/entry-level roles).
3. Log in at `/accounts/login/` with your email and password.

### Profile & Skills Wizard
1. Open **My Profile & Skills** (`/accounts/profile/`).
2. Fill in:
   - **Headline** (e.g. *Aspiring Full-Stack Django Developer*).
   - **Target Career Role** (e.g. *Python Backend Engineer*).
   - **Bio / Summary** and Social Profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio).
3. Add your **Technical & Soft Skills** with proficiency levels (Beginner, Intermediate, Expert).
4. Add your **Academic History** (University, degree, field of study, graduation year).
5. Add any past **Work / Internship Experiences**.

---

## 2. AI Career Recommendations

1. Click **Career Recommendations** in the sidebar (`/careers/`).
2. Select your work preference (Hybrid, Remote, Onsite) and target industries.
3. Click **Generate AI Career Recommendations**.
4. The system will synthesize your academic record and skills using Google Gemini to present:
   - **Top Matching Career Tracks** in Bangladesh.
   - **Readiness Rating & Suitability Rationale**.
   - **Diagnosed Missing Skills** with concrete recommended next steps.

---

## 3. AI Resume Studio & PDF Export

1. Open **AI Resume Studio** (`/resumes/`).
2. Click **Create New Resume** to initialize a CV pre-populated from your profile.
3. Use the interactive split-screen editor:
   - Click **Generate Summary with AI** to synthesize an ATS-optimized executive summary based strictly on your verified facts.
   - In Experience & Projects: click **Polish with AI** to strengthen action verbs and grammar.
4. Preview your resume in real time.
5. Click **Download PDF** to export an ATS-compliant, professional PDF rendered with ReportLab.

---

## 4. AI Career Advisor Chatbot

1. Open **Career Advisor Chat** (`/chat/`).
2. Ask any question regarding career navigation, interview tactics, or salary trends in Dhaka.
3. The chatbot incorporates your background context to deliver hyper-specific advice.
4. Multi-turn history is automatically saved in your consultation archive.
5. Use **New Chat** anytime to begin a clean conversation topic.

---

## 5. Job Matches & Deterministic Fit

1. Open **Job Matches** (`/jobs/`).
2. Browse curated openings in Bangladesh.
3. Review your **Match Percentage** on each job card:
   - Calculated deterministically by comparing your skills against required (80%) and preferred (15%) job skills.
   - Click on any job to view the exact breakdown of **Matched Skills** vs. **Missing Skills**.
4. Click the bookmark icon to save jobs to your **Saved Bookmarks** (`/jobs/saved/`).

---

## 6. Mock Interview Arena

1. Open **Mock Interview Arena** (`/interviews/`).
2. Select your target position, difficulty level (Junior, Mid, Senior), and question type (Technical, HR, Mixed).
3. Click **Launch Interview Session**.
4. Answer each question in the text arena.
5. Submit your answer to receive instant rubric evaluation:
   - **Score out of 10**.
   - **Identified Strengths**.
   - **Concrete Areas for Improvement**.
   - **Key Concepts an Ideal Answer Would Cover**.
6. View your **Performance Summary** upon completing all questions.

---

## 7. Learning Roadmaps & Progress Tracking

1. Open **Learning Roadmaps** (`/roadmaps/`).
2. Click **Generate New Roadmap** and enter your desired role.
3. CareerMate AI architectures a **3-stage progressive curriculum** (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
4. Each stage outlines actionable milestones with hands-on mini-projects and curated learning links.
5. Check off completed milestones to update your visual progress bar in real time.
6. Explore curated platform learning materials in the resource library.

---

## 8. Platform Support & Moderation

- Encountered an inaccurate AI output, broken job link, or have general feedback?
- Click **Report & Feedback** (`/roadmaps/report/`) to file a moderation ticket directly to the platform administrators.
